How the Specialty Coffee Swarm fits together

bean-core is the shared foundation of the swarm. It owns no feature code: it defines which stages exist and in what order (stages.yaml), who orchestrates them (@expo), how every artifact proves where it came from (OKF v0.2 stamping), and what is never allowed (git guardrails). The other plugins plug their skills into the stages it declares.
